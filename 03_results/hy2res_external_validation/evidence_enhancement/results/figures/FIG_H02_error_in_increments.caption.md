**FIG_H02_error_in_increments.** ECDFs of absolute 5-s-ahead pressure errors divided by the 0.1 bar recording increment. Persistence errors are exactly 0 or 1 increment; Ridge errors are continuous, never 0, and mostly below one increment.

Scientific question: How large are errors relative to the recording grid?

Source data: `test_predictions.csv.gz`

Reproduction function: `report.fig_h02`
