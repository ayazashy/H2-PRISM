# H₂-PRISM integrated validation — Limitations

| Area | Limitation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Center | One controlled simulation family; air-like gas model; 0 verified independent generating families; 12 captures | Mechanism, location and D(Y) results are controlled-simulation evidence only. There is no hydrogen-material or field claim. |
| Center D(Y) | Bounds are hypothetical. 3 of 9 declared hypotheses have no adapter. The 672 cells are repeated views of 6 captures. | D(Y) is conditionally demonstrated. It is not certified, and its outcomes are counts, not rates. |
| R2 / H₂-SimNet | All outer-test windows have been inspected. 36/57 runs have disputed attribution (D1 not recorded). 17 scenario ancestors are shared by all topologies. Thin classes. | H1 is not estimable. Every reanalysis is exploratory and confounded by the defect. |
| MHC | One apparatus; three held-out runs; serially dependent rows; set points not recorded; no event labels | The reference benchmark is valid only within scope. No diagnosis is possible. |
| HY2RES | One system and one test period; power-defined states; no fault labels; undefined columns excluded; timezone unspecified | Results cover evidence validity and reference behaviour. They are not fault detection. |
| Recording grids | Instrument resolution and accuracy are undocumented in both experimental sources | Errors are read against the recording increment, never against instrument accuracy. |
| Cross-study | Different systems, units and evidence levels | Results are shown side by side and never pooled. |
| Figures | Automated QA plus visual inspection at reduced size | Journal acceptance is not guaranteed. A journal may still require layout changes. |
