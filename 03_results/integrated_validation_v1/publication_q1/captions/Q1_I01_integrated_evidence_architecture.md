# Q1_I01 — Evidence architecture

**Scientific question.** How do the studies contribute to each evidence layer?

**Caption.** Evidence sources (columns) and evidence layers (rows) of the integrated package. Layer I: source identity, measurement and recording qualification. Layer II: reference behaviour and evidence validity. Layer III: diagnostic decisions. Dashed boxes: blocked by evidence (no qualified labels, bounds or locations). Numbers are taken from T01–T10 and the H1 report; studies are never pooled.

**Interpretation limit.** Studies differ in system, data type and evidence level and are not pooled; simulated (Center, H2-SimNet), experimental (MHC) and operational (HY2RES) evidence is reported side by side only.
