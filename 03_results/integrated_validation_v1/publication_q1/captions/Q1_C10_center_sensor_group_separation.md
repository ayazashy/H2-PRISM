# Q1_C10 — Sensor-group dependence of outlet separation

**Scientific question.** Which sensor group carries the separation between outlets?

**Caption.** B3 contrast between withdrawal templates for each outlet pair and sensor group (±1 s, 30 s prefix). The flow share of the all-sensor contrast is ≥ 1.000 for every pair. Pressure-only contrasts are two to three decades smaller, and withholding MFS3 collapses the SID 213 vs SID 215 contrast, because SID 215 is flow-blind.

**Interpretation limit.** B3 normalizes by absolute channel level; equal channel weights.
