# Advanced validation — Limitations

1. **R2 analyses** are EXPLORATORY_POST_HOC.
   - All windows were inspected, there are only 17 templates, and labels are disputed for 36/57 runs.
   - Window counts are dependent and are not independent trials.
2. **Center formal results** are CONDITIONAL_HYPOTHETICAL_BOUNDS on one air-like simulation family with six queries.
   - The 2,376 decisions are deliberately varied views of the same captures. They are counts, not rates.
3. **Localization** rests on one supported outlet represented by two near-duplicate captures.
4. **Timing.** Jitter was not studied. Delays were applied by shifting the recorded series, which assumes a pure transport-like lag.
5. **Isolability** is template-level under declared bounds. It is not structural, physical or field isolability.
6. **Benchmarks.** One laptop-class CPU was used, and memory counts Python allocations only. D(x) was timed on synthetic arrays.
7. **Gas transfer and cost:** no diagnostic or monetary result exists, only qualification and parametric structure.
8. **Literature.** DOIs come from search listings and require publisher verification. Metrics were not extracted beyond abstracts or listings.
9. **Provenance gap.** The HY2RES model-training code is not tracked in this branch.
