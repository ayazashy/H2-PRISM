# V2-F04 — Sensor–hypothesis incidence

**Scientific question.** Which channels respond to each mechanism by more than the REF bound?

**Caption.** Peak absolute template residual per channel divided by 2 ε_REF. The SID 215 withdrawal exceeds 2 ε_REF on no channel, which explains why it cannot be told apart from normal operation at REF; the SID 213 and SID 312 withdrawals are carried by their branch flow meters (MFS3, MFS1).

**Interpretation limit.** Peak magnitude is a necessary but not sufficient condition for isolability (shifts and rival templates matter: see V2-F03).
