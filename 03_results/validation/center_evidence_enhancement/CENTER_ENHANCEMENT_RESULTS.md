# Center evidence enhancement — Results

Every new number below comes from `analysis/` (generated by `h2prism.center_enhancement`) or from frozen artifacts named in place. Full tables are in `tables/` (CSV and Markdown) and are bundled in `tables_bundle.md`. Figures are standalone PNG (3840 × 2160) and SVG pairs in `figures/`, each with a caption file. ## 3.1 Source and experiment qualification

**Question.** What was actually tested, and how independent is it?

**Data.** 12 accepted captures (6 matched pairs). Each was re-verified against its receipt hash, with an exact 0.2 s clock, 100,001 samples from 0 to 20,000 s, and 13 sensors (PS1–PS10 in MPa, MFS1–MFS3 in kg/s). All derive from one source model, `Normal_HNetwork_gest_center.slx`. Its gas block has **R = 0.287 kJ/(kg·K)**, which is air-like, read directly from the model file. The Center results are therefore controlled air-like simulation evidence, not hydrogen-pipeline evidence (TAB_C01).

**Interventions, from each capture's `design.json`:**
- Outlet-area step from 0.0010 to 0.0011 m² at 1,000 s at three atmospheric outlets:
  - SID 213 → reservoir SID 200, branch flow sensor SID 218 (= MFS3);
  - SID 312 → reservoir SID 316, branch sensor SID 311 (= MFS1);
  - **SID 215 → reservoir SID 217, with no branch flow sensor.**
- The same SID 213 step under a +0.005 MPa reservoir overlay (the alternate profile).
- A −0.17 MPa internal pressure-command overlay at SID 55 at 1,000 s.
- A command-issuance step-time shift at SID 59 from 25 s to 26 s. This is command issuance, not actuator lag.

**Dependence.** All 12 captures share one source family; there are zero verified independent generating families. The SID 213, SID 55 and SID 59 controls are bitwise identical. PS3 and PS10 are bitwise identical in every capture. PS5 and PS7 show zero response to every withdrawal.

Figure: `FIG_C01_intervention_observability`.

## 3.2 Mechanism recognition without self-templates

**Question.** Does mechanism recognition survive when each query's own template is removed?

**Method.** Frozen B3 kernel, all sensors, 30 s prefix, ±1 s shift. 7 capture-derived queries span 4 configured mechanisms; the all-zero QUIET construct is excluded from counts.

**Baseline.** In the frozen library, all 7 queries match a self-template (5) or a near-duplicate template (2).

**Results** (TAB_C02, TAB_C02b; FIG_C02, FIG_C03):

| Query | Nearest mechanism, own template excluded | B3 score | Runner-up (margin) | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| SID 213 withdrawal | Normal (zero residual) | 0.028358 | Withdrawal via SID 215 (7.4e-5) | incorrect |
| SID 312 withdrawal | Withdrawal via SID 215 | 0.029388 | Normal (1.9e-5) | correct, near tie |
| SID 215 withdrawal | Normal | 0.002185 | Timing shift (0.0094); withdrawal ranks 3rd | incorrect |
| SID 213, alt. profile | Withdrawal via SID 213 | 2.8e-5 | Normal (0.028) | correct, near duplicate |
| Nominal start-up | Normal via another control run | 1e-6 | Withdrawal (0.25) | correct, near duplicate |
| SID 55 pressure command | (own mechanism absent) nearest: Timing | 0.036924 | — | unsupported; gate blocks |
| SID 59 timing shift | (own mechanism absent) nearest: Withdrawal | 0.011633 | — | unsupported; **gate routes it** |

**Agreement:** 3/5 supported queries, 2 unsupported. Two of the three agreements rest on near-duplicate templates, and the third is a 1.9e-5 near tie.

**Per-outlet behaviour.** For SID 213 and SID 312, the nearest other-outlet withdrawal and the zero residual differ by only 0.26 % and 0.06 %. SID 215 is 12.8× closer to the zero residual than to any other withdrawal.

**Sensor groups.** Under pressure-only or all-minus-MFS3 sensing, SID 215 becomes correctly *withdrawal* (4/5); SID 213 stays incorrect in every group. The 0 s clock bound gives identical statuses.

**Interpretation.** In this sensor configuration a withdrawal has no outlet-independent signature. Each outlet excites a different instrument, so "withdrawal" recognition in the frozen screen was template identity matching. The pressure-command rival stays distinguishable from withdrawals (its nearest is the other operational rival). The timing case remains the open-set failure: with its own template absent it is routed to a withdrawal outlet.

**Limitation.** One source family; 7 queries; descriptive counts only.

## 3.4 Sensor-dependent identifiability

**Question.** Which measurements carry the outlet information?

**Results** (TAB_C04; FIG_C05, FIG_C06):

| Outlet pair | Largest pressure difference | Largest flow difference | B3 flow share (all sensors) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SID 213 – SID 312 | 1.15 kPa | 0.691 kg/s | 0.999997 |
| SID 213 – SID 215 | 5.47 kPa | 0.691 kg/s (MFS3 only) | 0.99987 |
| SID 312 – SID 215 | 4.80 kPa | 0.647 kg/s (MFS1 only) | 0.99988 |
| SID 213 – alt. SID 213 | 0.00245 kPa | 0.000684 kg/s | 0.99998 |

- The frozen figures are verified: B3 flow-only 0.0843 versus pressure-only 7.99e-5 for SID 213–SID 312, and the full-library mechanism margin of 0.02836 falls to 0.00014 without MFS3 (frozen TAB_09).
- Per channel, SID 213 moves MFS3 by 0.691 kg/s, SID 312 moves MFS1 by 0.647 kg/s, and SID 215 moves no flow meter by more than 3.9e-4 kg/s. SID 215 appears as 5.6 kPa at PS4/PS8 and 3.8 kPa at PS9. PS4 sits at junction SID 109 upstream of SID 215.
- Runner-up/correct separation for the SID 213 queries:
  - 1,030× with all sensors;
  - 1,030× flow-only;
  - **569× pressure-only**;
  - 2,676× without MFS3. Removing MFS3 cuts the absolute margin 0.028 → 0.0021, but the near-duplicate match improves more.

**Interpretation.**
- B3's flow dominance partly reflects its normalization by absolute operating level (~6 MPa). In native units the outlets differ by 1.1–5.5 kPa in pressure, and pressure alone still ranks SID 213 first.
- Outlet separability is therefore **sensor-coverage-dependent**: two outlets are flow-identified by their own branch meters, while SID 215 is flow-unobservable and pressure-identified only.
- Whether pressure-only distinction is usable depends on pressure precision (§3.5).

## 3.5 Conditional D(Y) exclusion behaviour — CONDITIONAL_HYPOTHETICAL_BOUNDS

**Question.** Under which declared bounds is each supported rival excluded, and is there a bound range where only the configured outlet survives?

**Results** (TAB_C05; FIG_C07, FIG_C08). Onsets are exact, with ±1 s clock, 30 s prefix, and ε = ε_obs + ε_model.

| Query | Sensing | Configured hypothesis feasible from F | Smallest rival exclusion onset E (rival) | Correct-SPECIFIC window |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| SID 213 | pressure | 2.45e-6 MPa | 5.14e-4 MPa (Normal) | [2.45e-6, 5.14e-4) MPa, ×210 |
| SID 213 | flow | 6.84e-4 kg/s | 0.6913 kg/s (W·SID 312) | [6.84e-4, 0.691) kg/s, ×1,011 |
| SID 213 alt. | pressure | 2.45e-6 MPa | 5.17e-4 MPa (Normal) | ×211 |
| SID 213 alt. | flow | 6.83e-4 kg/s | 0.6919 kg/s | ×1,012 |
| SID 312 | pressure / flow | unsupported | 1.17e-3 MPa / 0.647 kg/s | none |
| SID 215 | pressure / flow | unsupported | 4.82e-3 MPa / **3.85e-4 kg/s (Normal)** | none |
| SID 55 command | pressure / flow | unsupported | 0.170 MPa / 0.0645 kg/s | none |
| SID 59 timing | pressure / flow | unsupported | 0.168 MPa / 0.0758 kg/s | none |

- **Monotonicity is proved, not assumed**: exclusion is non-increasing and feasibility non-decreasing in ε. The engine agreed with the closed form in 3,584/3,584 checks, and the split between observation and model bound never changed a decision.
- The indeterminate band [E, F) is empty for most rival pairs. Non-empty bands come from the clock-shift hull, where a single-coordinate certificate is weaker than a per-shift check. Examples: the timing rival ([0.151, 0.168) MPa) and the configured SID 213 hypothesis in pressure ([8.8e-7, 2.45e-6) MPa). With all sensors no grid scenario falls inside a band. In single-group views, 5 of 336 restricted cells fall inside one and return UNRESOLVED / INDETERMINATE_HYPOTHESES: SID 213 and its alternate profile at S1 in pressure-only and all-minus-MFS3, and SID 215 at S5 in flow-only.
- **At the observed solver-refinement scale** (REF: 0.034 MPa, 0.018 kg/s):
  - pressure exclusion of every withdrawal rival is lost (all E < 5.6e-3 MPa);
  - flow exclusion of SID 213's rivals survives.

**Interpretation.** The frozen engine shows conditional exclusion behaviour. It would isolate the SID 213 withdrawal if pressure were bounded below ~0.5 kPa or flow below ~0.69 kg/s, with model discrepancy included in the bound, given a valid same-outlet prediction. The correct-outlet onset (2.45e-6 MPa, 6.8e-4 kg/s) is set by the profile change between the two SID 213 realizations. It is a lower bound on the model discrepancy any prediction would carry, not a qualified enclosure.

## 3.6 Specific, ambiguous and unresolved decisions

**Results** (TAB_C06; FIG_C09, FIG_C10). All sensors, ±1 s, 6 residual queries × 7 scenarios = 42 cells per universe.

| Evidence condition | SPECIFIC correct | SPECIFIC wrong | AMBIGUOUS | UNRESOLVED (reason) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Frozen formal result (12 observations) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 12 (INVALID_UNCERTAINTY; every hypothesis adapter = None) |
| Conditional, full declared universe | 0 | 0 | 0 | 42 (INDETERMINATE_HYPOTHESES) |
| Conditional, restricted supported universe | 8 | 2 | 4 | 28 (MODEL_MISMATCH) |

**Frozen result.**
- The frozen 12/12 UNRESOLVED had **two independent blockers**: empty error vectors (`INVALID_UNCERTAINTY`) and no hypothesis adapters.
- Supplying hypothetical bounds alone would not have changed it.

**Full declared universe.**
- 336/336 cells are UNRESOLVED over all groups and clock bounds.
- The cause is that SID 222, COMPRESSOR_FAULT and ACTUATOR_DELAY are declared but unmodelled.
- AMBIGUOUS is unreachable while any rival is indeterminate.

**Restricted universe.**
- **Correct SPECIFIC:** all 8 cells are the two SID 213 queries in S3–S5 and REF.
- **AMBIGUOUS:** occurs only at S6. The configured hypothesis is retained for SID 213 and its alternate profile. It is outside the universe for SID 312 and SID 215.
- **UNRESOLVED:** every one of the 28 is MODEL_MISMATCH, meaning all supported hypotheses were excluded. That is the correct signal when the true mechanism is absent, or the bounds are tighter than template discrepancy.
- **Wrong SPECIFIC:** the 2 wrong cells are **SID 215 → "Normal"** at S5 and REF. A flow-blind outlet withdrawal is confidently classed as normal once ε_F ≥ 3.85e-4 kg/s, because pressure no longer excludes Normal.
- In flow-only sensing, the timing case is also wrongly SPECIFIC ("pressure command") at S5.
- No hypothesis with an adapter was ever INDETERMINATE in the all-sensor view.

## 3.7 Comparison with the original research questions

- **RQ3.** Experiment E is the first engine-level demonstration of all three outcomes on Center data, under declared bounds. It also shows the outcome semantics depend on the declared universe: the restricted universe yields wrong SPECIFIC decisions when the true mechanism is unrepresented.
- **Archive classification (Experiment F).**
  - The H₂-SimNet event folders `anom_gestcent` and `anom_gestdown` carry each other's source model: 36 of 57 runs, 32 eligible. This explains 99.5 % of R2 errors: Center LEAK → NORMAL in 7,307 windows, Downstream NORMAL → LEAK/FAULT in 665.
  - Upstream results are unaffected. The registered RQ1 status stands.
  - Owner decision **D1 (A/B/C) is not recorded**. The corrected v0.3 replication was **not run** (`EXPERIMENT_F_PROPOSAL.md`).

## 3.8 Scientific limitations

- One air-like source family with zero independent generating families. All counts are descriptive.
- Anchors are retrospective. Bounds are hypothetical, not qualified sensor or model specifications.
- The only same-outlet replicate is a near-duplicate. SID 312 and SID 215 cannot be evaluated as locations.
- No FAULT or actuator-DELAY truth, no geometry, and no hydrogen material fidelity.
- Nothing here establishes industrial leak localization, field fault diagnosis or selective-risk improvement.
