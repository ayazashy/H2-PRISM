**Methodology.** We compared two checksum-verified Center simulations on 100,001 exact shared times with 13 native sensors. The engineered case increased the received area of an existing atmospheric outlet from 0.0010 to 0.0011 m^2 at 1,000 s. The paired NORMAL retained 0.0010 m^2. We applied event-aware quadrature to the six signed boundary flows and reconstructed mass from eight storage components.

**Results.** Outlet flow increased by 0.6913 kg/s at the first saved opening sample, peaked at 0.6945 kg/s above control, and produced 13,064.8 kg of extra withdrawal. PS1 and MFS3 gave the earliest descriptive evidence. Event-aware final mass residuals were -0.939 kg for NORMAL and -1.306 kg for opening.

**Contribution.** The experiment links a traced outlet command to observed area, pressure, signed flow, and storage on the native clock. It reports numerical separability and then applies the existing hypothesis-consistency and conditional localization rules to the same provenance-bound data.

**Conclusion.** The modeled intervention is descriptively separable from its matched control. H2-PRISM returns UNRESOLVED and L0 because qualified uncertainty and competing prediction enclosures are absent. The results apply to one controlled Center simulation family.
