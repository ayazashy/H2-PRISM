# Evidence-grounded pipeline event diagnosis with H₂-PRISM

**Question** Can pressure and mass-flow observations support a unique diagnosis when controlled mechanisms overlap?

**Methods** Twelve paired Center simulations and archived causal replay. Time-aligned matched pressure/flow residuals were assessed against all available mechanism templates. Qualified hypothesis decisions required measurement and model bounds. The replay used fixed chronological alarm policies.

**Results** A one-second command shift produced a 228× clock-versus-event residual RMS contrast. Removing MFS3 reduced a full-library margin from 0.02836 to 0.00014. Eligibility screening changed wrong timing routes from 165 to 164 among 1,792 repeated settings and was not adopted. All 12 formal decisions were unresolved with L0 localization. The clean one-second EWMA detected 39/48 archived events and had 42 false starts over 65.63 inactive archive hours.

**Conclusion** Matched observations reveal controlled mechanism differences. The Center evidence supports exploratory ranking and explicit abstention. Independent diagnostic accuracy, localization and added selective-risk value remain unestablished.
