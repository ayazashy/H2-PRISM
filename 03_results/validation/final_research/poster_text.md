# H2-PRISM research poster text

**Introduction and gap.** Pipeline sensor departures can leave multiple physical hypotheses compatible. H2-PRISM asks whether the evidence justifies a specific diagnosis and conditional location rather than relying on classifier confidence alone.

**Methodology.** Original Center source trajectories were verified against their solver archive and numerical refinement studies. We use 13 synchronous sensors, signed external flows and eight storage histories in checksum-traced, paired outlet simulations, with each intervention compared against its own control on the saved clock.

**Results.** SID 213 opened from 0.0010 to 0.0011 m^2 at 1,000 s. Outlet flow grew by 0.6913 kg/s at onset and by 0.6945 kg/s at peak, yielding 13,064.8 kg of extra withdrawal. Event-aware residuals were -0.939 kg and -1.306 kg for its control and opening.
SID312 produced +0.6468 kg/s at onset and 12,139.5 kg of extra outlet withdrawal.
SID215 produced +0.6005 kg/s at onset and 11,043.4 kg of extra outlet withdrawal.

**Contribution.** The study ties observed command, pressure, signed flow and stored mass to the original model identity and distinguishes descriptive sensor evidence from certified hypothesis exclusions.

**Conclusion.** The controlled responses are numerical exploratory evidence. Missing qualified uncertainty and model prediction sets leave the formal result UNRESOLVED and L0. Independent accuracy and hydrogen-specific physical fidelity remain unverified.

**Future work.** Validate a matched operational pressure-command rival, establish a boundary-only operating profile, and qualify physical uncertainty and independent generating families before calibrated abstention comparisons.
