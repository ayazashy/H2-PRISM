## Limits and next experiment

The timing intervention shifts command issuance. It supplies neither a model of actuator-response DELAY nor a compressor FAULT mechanism. The controlled outlets are atmospheric withdrawal cases. Confirmatory four-class training, independent calibration, conditional localization accuracy, cross-topology generalization and industrial validation are blocked by zero verified independent generating families. The exact next source work is an auditable actuator/command-response pair with measured nominal lag and a separately qualified equipment-fault mechanism with validated failure envelope and observed device state. Then reserve independent development, calibration and untouched test roots with all four classes and location candidates. Use the [pilot gate](../phase13_pilot_qualification/report.md) for structural admission and qualify observation, clock, initial/boundary, parameter and prediction bounds before formal evaluation. Source gas fidelity and the clean-release reconciliation remain open.
