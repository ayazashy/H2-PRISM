# NRES v1 controlled research report

**Decision — RETAIN_AS_EQUIVALENT_BASELINE.** Retain time-aligned matched-reference residual RMS (B3) as the primary comparison. The NRES weighting increases some numerical margins, but it yields no new correct top rank, no calibrated set narrowing, and no formal improvement on this evidence. B4 uses different weights and is not algebraically identical to B3 with both sensor types present.

## Method and evidence

Rechecked all 12 raw capture SHA-256 values against accepted receipts and verified the common 0.2 s saved clock, declared pressure and signed mass-flow units and finite observations. The six matched experiments share one Center source family. Canonical inputs use MPa and kg/s. Sensor IDs, roles and units are explicit and arbitrary counts are allowed. Independent families remain zero. No source or benchmark was modified.

For a sensor prefix at declared deadline, subtract a contemporaneous matched control where the experiment provides one. For each hypothesis calculate the square root of the weighted mean squared difference after dividing by fixed per-channel scales. Minimize over one common relative translation on the saved grid. All candidates and shifts share valid support. B0 uses raw and exact alignment, B1 residual and exact alignment, B2 raw and shift, B3 residual and shift, B4 the B3 residual and shift with equal total pressure and flow weights. The normalizer is the nominal SID 213 control RMS before 1,000 s. It was not fitted to the alternate query. Geometry also compares fixed physical scalings of 1 MPa and 1 kg/s. No covariance fit, per-channel shifts, amplitude fitting or warping. A fractional bound of ±0.5 s admits ±0.4 s on-grid with no interpolation. Sub-grid sensitivity remains unmeasured. The candidate-set policy retains all candidates and labels narrowing uncalibrated.

For operational use, observations and nominal references are clipped by the decision deadline and must share a valid clock/schema. Existing data provide a matched counterfactual only in the controlled setting. The operational gate returns BLOCKED_REFERENCE without a separately qualified reference. Have any caller qualify the declared nominal model and sensor mapping before enabling it. No operational ranking metric is reported.

## Four completed experiments

**A. Timing.** Independently reproduced the SID 59 published values: clock-aligned 30 s RMS **0.027781127**, and retrospective onset-aligned RMS **0.000121924**, a **227.9×** reduction. That earlier comparison starts each window at its own event knot. On a separate fixed query window beginning at 25 s, a bounded ±1 s search chose a −1 s template shift. B0 and B1 gave **0.027781127**, B2 and B3 **0.000130443**, B4 **0.000191082**. The same constant nominal reference was subtracted from both timing trajectories, so B0 equals B1 and B2 equals B3 by algebra. A 0.5 s bound only permits 0.4 s and leaves B3 at **0.020494**. The window difference explains why the fixed-window optimum differs slightly from the earlier oracle-event comparison. Ordinary shared alignment explains the reduction. B4 reweights the residual magnitude without recovering a better shift.

**B. Location and mechanism geometry.** At 30 s and ±1 s, B3 all-sensor RMS distances were **0.040499** for 213/312, **0.028433** for 213/215 and **0.029388** for 312/215. B4 gives **0.059613**, **0.041850** and **0.043256**, preserving the order with a different norm. Location and event magnitude cannot be separated fully from these cases. The pressure-command rival retains measurable contrast to the outlet templates. Alignment can also erase useful differences: B3 SID 215/timing drops from **0.026891** to **0.011633** (56.7%). That compression warrants caution about over-alignment; no qualified harmful-over-alignment rate can be estimated. The fixed-physical-unit geometry and leave-one-out matrix are supplied.

**C. Alternate profile.** The previously inspected alternate-profile query is excluded from the template library. Traces are compared from its causal alert at 1,000 s and at 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 s prefixes, using only observed knots through each deadline. The 560 settings per method are dependent sensitivity contrasts on one query. SID 213 ranked first in **555/560 B0**, **560/560 B1**, **550/560 B2**, **560/560 B3**, and **560/560 B4**. Thus matching the reference improved ranking relative to raw data on some settings. B4 did not improve over matched residual baselines. At 30 s, ±1 s, all channels, B3 and B4 margins were **0.028432** and **0.041849**. Scope and units are comparable; the larger B4 margin follows a changed weighting and is not a gain in decision accuracy.

**D. Negative controls.** With a deliberately incomplete location-only library, both B3 and B4 ranked **SID 215** first for operational pressure and command timing, and **SID 213** for the nominal pressure transient (30 s, ±1 s). They retained all candidates, so there was no certified or exploratory singleton. This exposes the risk of forcing top-1 when mechanisms outside the library are possible. In the alternate-profile stress test both methods kept SID 213 first at ±1 s under assumed white and correlated noise, pressure bias, frozen/missing MFS3 and a mismatched reference. These were deterministic single-case stresses, not tolerances. Noise innovation and pressure bias were 1% of nominal channel RMS; lag-one coefficient 0.8. The tested stuck value is the first query residual, which is already close to its short plateau. A stable rank in that fixture is weak evidence about actual sensor failures.

## Sensor contribution and computational cost

The original full-mechanism library reproduces **0.028358 → 0.000141** on MFS3 removal. NORMAL is runner-up in both cases. The location-only margin is different: B3 falls **0.028432 → 0.002125**, and B4 **0.041849 → 0.003643** when the declared subset is rebalanced. For B3 the retained pressure-only/flow-only margins are **0.00007987 / 0.059183**. Pressure-only and flow-only B3/B4 use the same relative weights. Missing MFS3 stress keeps the declared weights and masks its entries; it does not rebalance groups. This is substantial context-specific evidence leverage, not causal necessity, unique information or an industrial placement finding.

On this system, the 13-channel, three-template, ±2 s calculation at 151 knots took **1.396 ms median**, **1.781 ms p95**, **2.636 ms p99** over 30 calls, with about **1.08 MB traced transient allocation**. With a zero bound it took **0.413 ms median**. This measures the local numeric kernel, not acquisition or decision delay, and excludes the retained template library. B3 and B4 share the identical grid search and asymptotic cost; B4 adds one diagonal weighting policy. Neither has an independently calibrated cutoff. The result table varies channels, candidate count and offset count.

## Scientific foundation and gates

Residual generation and signatures build on analytical redundancy rather than originating here. Geometric diagnosis work relates residual generators to behavioral sets; this experiment instead computes finite-library distances. Fagarasan, Ploix and Gentil's set-membership detection considers bounded model and measurement uncertainty. The exploratory RMS minimum does not supply such bounds. Kościelny and Sztyber-Betley treat isolability as dependent on the diagnostic representation, signals and assumptions. The specific contribution here is a controlled comparison of simple diagonal weighting and time translation, with negative controls. No new theorem or novelty claim is established. [1987 process diagnosis survey](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1474667017558243) · [Sheikhi et al. geometric diagnosis review](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-control-030123-015422) · [Set-membership detection and isolation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109804001967) · [Isolability assessment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2025.112979)

G1 implementation tested. G2 timing reduction established within the simulation pair. G3 nonzero mechanism contrasts remain. G4 alignment compression and forced nearest-site errors warrant caution. G5 fair B3/B4 comparison completed. G6 sensor contribution interpretable within scope. G7 ranking stable but no gain over B3. G8 operational reference blocked with deadline tests. G9 no formal exclusions introduced. G10 compute measured without evidence that added weighting improves decisions.

The formal engine is unchanged. Results remain **12 UNRESOLVED, zero qualified exclusions, L0**, and exact joint set-membership equivalence under identical evidence. No formal added value, independent localization accuracy, industrial transfer, or matched-coverage risk improvement has been demonstrated. A different network can reuse unit handling, schema, prefix clipping, mask and shared shift math. It needs physically available nominal references, compatible sensor roles and template clocks, new calibration and independent evidence.

**Poster text.** In one controlled gas-network family, shared time alignment reduced a one-second command-shift contrast by roughly 228×. A held-template alternate-profile outlet query favored nominal SID 213 under both ordinary residual matching and group-weighted NRES. Non-withdrawal controls exposed forced location ranks. NRES supplied interpretable sensitivity outputs but no incremental ranking or formal decision gain.
