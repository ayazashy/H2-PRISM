# Measurement and experiment audit

The Experiment Overview and all 29 workbooks passed inventory and layout inspection. The table records raw row counts, timestamp flags, missing selected signals, wall-clock span, initial-state notes and documented operating settings. The full LabVIEW block is identified by the Date, Time and Seconds header on each Cycle Definition worksheet. Dedicated measurements stored under alternate loading-station labels are outside the selected channel map. A count of invalid values for an absent named channel therefore represents the absence of that named signal, not necessarily a failed instrument. [Experiment registry](experiment_registry.csv) · [Workbook layouts and recorded units](channel_map.json)

LabVIEW pressure channels P-1 and P-2 refer to Stages I and II. MFC-1, MFC-2 and MFC-3 refer to the inlet, interstage path and outlet. The Cycle Data sheets document pressure in barg and MFC readings in ln/min. Temperature context includes the two same-logger heat-transfer-fluid temperatures in °C. Separately timed Graphtec and Picolog records are excluded from predictors because row correspondence alone does not establish decision-time availability. Cycle Data is a representative-cycle view. Heat, mass-balance, efficiency and summary sheets contain derived results and are excluded from model inputs. Raw signals were parsed with formulas disabled as inputs, treating formula text and spreadsheet errors as nonnumeric. [Workbook layouts](channel_map.json)

The eight eligible runs have median wall-clock intervals from 1.0000 to 1.0000 seconds. Scoring requires a consecutive Seconds counter and a compatible wall-clock interval. Exact duplicate timestamps, missing time and readings, and intervals outside the fixed tolerance are captured in the audit. No external sensor calibration or trigger synchronization was established. [Experiment registry](experiment_registry.csv) · [Frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md)

Recording dates and workbook notes determine conservative groups. Run 25 inherits the Stage I charge from run 5. Runs 26 and 27 share a continuous history, as explicitly described in run 27. Experiment 27's Cycle Definition duplicates 15-minute text while the Overview assigns a 10-minute half-cycle. The inventory and figure use the Overview convention, and no settings enter the models.
