# Frozen Study B protocol

The task is nominal one-second-ahead prediction of Stage II hydrogen gauge pressure (P-2, barg) and the outlet hydrogen flow meter/controller reading (MFC-3, ln/min) on the dual-stage compressor. Experiments 1–21 cover thermal, capacity, or single-stage modes with different physical meanings or flow paths. The workbook documents normalized volumetric flow. Results retain that scale because a conversion standard and mass calibration were not established.

Development runs are 22, 23, 25, 26, and 27. Held-out runs are 24, 28, and 29. The 12 May recordings are grouped conservatively. The 18 July runs were recorded consecutively and stay together. The 22 July recordings stay together even though their starting states are described as undefined. Run 25 starts with charge inherited from run 5, which is outside the benchmark. The test includes the 180 bar outlet condition for each Stage I material and the 20 bar inlet condition for MmNiFe. The comparison is descriptive given the shared apparatus and unresolved independence across dates.

Pair only adjacent LabVIEW rows whose seconds counter increments by one and whose wall-clock difference is 0.8–1.2 s inclusive. Both pressure and flow must be finite at both endpoints. Exclude invalid intervals, retain finite zero readings, and use no interpolation or cross-run state. Whole recorded sequences include startup and nominal transitions. Availability of the current recorded row is assumed at decision time.

B0 predicts the current observation. B1 adds a ridge estimate of the next-row change using only the current pressure and outlet flow. Standardize the two inputs on concatenated development pairs and fit with alpha 100 and an intercept. The candidate makes a single regularized correction to persistence with a small feature set suited to the three development recording groups. Operating settings are used for interpretation only. The output channels are evaluated in their own units with identical eligible rows, and the summary weights each held-out workbook equally. Runs 24 and 29 were selected for Figure 2 to contrast materials at the 180 bar outlet setting.
