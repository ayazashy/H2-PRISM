§3

Operating-envelope consistency

Reference calcs E, F and G evaluate the whole envelope (2–8 MPa × 283–313 K). No single operating point is extrapolated.

3.1 Capacity at 20 m/s, 313 K (kg/s)

Capacity is proportional to pressure, so a fixed demand that is feasible at 8 MPa can be infeasible at the lowest pressure. P4 therefore binds nominal demand to the lowest pressure and to every segment.

The earlier candidate nominal of 42.6 kg/s exceeds the 1.2 m capacity at 2 MPa at both 283 K and 313 K.

3.2 Leak severity across the envelope

Choked leak flow is proportional to upstream pressure. With areas sized once at 5 MPa:

Severity definition0.5 % class2 % class10 % classAssessment
A demand scales with capacity (∝ p)0.47–0.51 %1.89–2.02 %9.4–10.1 %Consistent within ±6 %
B demand fixed in kg/s×0.40–×1.670.8 %–3.3 %×0.40–×1.67Not consistent: severity mixes with operating point
Owner choice (P11)
  • (A) Severity = fraction of the run's actual local throughput; the leak area is recomputed per operating point.
  • (B) Severity = fixed area; the actual fraction is recorded per run as a label attribute.
  • Both are valid. (B) mixes severity with operating point. All leaks are choked at every pressure in the envelope.

3.3 Model form

The leak areas come from the isentropic nozzle formula with local hydrogen γ (1.41–1.43). Simscape's Local Restriction (G) uses a loss-coefficient model. Final areas must be confirmed against the Simscape block (V4, P16) at the decided operating points.

3.4 Compressor discharge temperature (released Δp = 4 MPa, isentropic)

Suction2 MPa3 MPa4 MPa6 MPa
283.15 K390 K363 K347 K330 K
313.15 K431 K401 K384 K364 K

Inside the property tables (≤ 500 K) but outside 270–330 K, so the aftercooler is necessary. These are ideal-gas isentropic estimates with mean local γ; the real-gas path is to be checked in V3.

3.5 Pressure drop

Frictional Δp per pipe is 14–85 kPa at 20 m/s and 2 MPa. Leak signatures in pressure are therefore small relative to the operating pressure. This is an input to the sensor-noise budget (specification §5), not a defect.

Evidence classification

Verified evidence

  • Released gas is a perfect, air-like gas (gas_spec = 1, R = 0.287)
  • Pipe (G) friction = Haaland, identical to the reference solver
  • Real-gas table interface and all units match
  • Released topology: vent, orifices, isentropic compressors, roughness, insulators, atmospheric initial state
  • Hydrogen calcs A–G under their stated approximations
  • V1 PASS; source-model hashes unchanged

Candidate recommendations

  • P2–P16 values and representations
  • Revised operating pressure 3–8 MPa
  • Per-segment demand limits at minimum pressure
  • Leak classes and the V4 acceptance amendment

Owner decisions

  • Every row in §1, by the named owner
  • Leak severity definition (A or B)
  • Inertia scope
  • Whether X06 may run on M1–M5 alone

MATLAB-dependent

  • R0 and V2–V7 runs
  • Final leak areas against the Simscape restriction
  • Transient segmentation
  • Run-time exposure of the real-gas parameters on the released block instance

Unresolved uncertainties

  • U-value and soil-temperature sources
  • Velocity-limit and roughness literature sources
  • Real-gas isentropic discharge path
  • Let-down representation
  • Executor designation under the grant
  • Memory sufficiency of the 8 GB laptop for Phase 3 compiles
