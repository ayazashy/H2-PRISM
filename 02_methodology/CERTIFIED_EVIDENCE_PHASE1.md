# Certified evidence core and H₂-SimNet feasibility

For a measurement box Y and declared hypothesis H, feasibility requires an admissible parameter witness whose joint output lies in Y. Exclusion requires a sound proof that the full reachable set misses Y. All remaining hypotheses are indeterminate. The three sets partition the declared hypotheses. A specific decision requires one feasible hypothesis and exclusion of every rival. Ambiguity requires multiple feasible hypotheses and no indeterminate rival. All exclusions with no witness indicate model mismatch.

The exact affine reference adapter checks candidates with rational arithmetic. It proves exclusion when one coordinate's full reachable interval is disjoint from its observed interval. Marginal overlap cannot establish joint feasibility. Nonconvergence and timeout provide no exclusion evidence. Exact arithmetic applies to the supplied numeric inputs and does not establish the accuracy of those inputs. A physical adapter must justify the forward map, admissible domain and uncertainty envelope against source-model evidence before its certificates carry physical meaning.

| Task | H₂-SimNet feasibility | Evidence and dependencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NORMAL | CONDITIONAL | Pressure and mass-flow telemetry and normal runs support interface checks. A validated intact-network envelope needs mapped pressure, signed boundary mass flows, temperature or justified thermal assumptions, valve and compressor states, initial conditions, geometry, gas properties and bounded model/sensor error. Raw solver exports have irregular timing and fewer flow channels. |
| LEAK | CONDITIONAL | Leak labels and pressure/flow responses support scenario construction. A transient inventory and mass-balance model needs signed boundary flows, operating inputs, geometry, gas properties, and source-verified leak onset, discharge parameters and bounds. Leak labels have documented compound and location conflicts. Deterministic uncertainty bounds are missing. |
| COMPRESSOR_FAULT | BLOCKED | C1-labeled runs exist, but physical exclusion needs the nominal compressor map/control logic, source-traced fault mechanism and admissible fault ranges, synchronized commands/states and suction/discharge telemetry or verified bounds for latent inputs. Available independent fault families are limited and mixed annotations require review. |
| DELAY | BLOCKED | CompressorDelay labels have finite onset and offset. The physical delay hypothesis needs source-verified command/response timing and delay insertion, timestamps and clock uncertainty, the baseline actuator dynamics and admissible lag bounds. The archive has one independent delay run per topology. |
| Leak-region localization | BLOCKED | Requires topology and geometry verified against the source blocks, label-to-region and sensor mappings, admissible leak parameters and uncertainty bounds, and proof that rivals are excluded after conditioning on leak. Filename and active location channels disagree in documented cases. |

Sources within the repository are the raw dataset verification report, particularly sections 4–9 and 12, and the H2Net v1 handoff START_HERE. The source report records unverified blend composition and clean/noise-added variants of shared simulation runs. The handoff records MATLAB structural and physical checks as unexecuted. The cited archive describes Gaussian noise, which supplies no hard finite error bound by itself. No class has a physically validated diagnosis from this Phase 1 reference implementation.

Preserve run-level independence groups and ancestry using the existing data contracts when building evaluation partitions. Keep physical model validation separate from diagnostic evaluation. Historical exploratory outcomes do not constitute new confirmatory evidence. As a first physical adapter step, trace the NORMAL boundary channels, operating inputs, gas properties and geometry to one source model and document deterministic uncertainty bounds before constructing a reachable-set enclosure.


The measurement contract uses the existing SensorRecord and its quantity/unit whitelist. It requires a documented clock source and a common sample grid. Cadence tolerance addresses spacing only. Phase 1 rejects shifted clocks and upstream resampling; a future adapter that accounts for timing uncertainty must explicitly propagate it in the model envelope.

For a future physical implementation, use the existing gas_physics property and flow functions where their assumptions apply. The design-freeze line_pack calculation and H2Net mass-balance sensor gates are references for building and verifying control volumes. They do not establish a transient reachable set. The Phase 1 adapter contains no residual heuristic and makes no gas-specific performance claim.
