**Done:**

**New in this execution:**
- `candidate/CANDIDATE_DESIGN.md` and `ENVELOPE_SCREENING.csv` (144 states on the 8 released pipes).
- Main finding: hydrogen's low density gives frictional pressure drops of only **14–85 kPa per pipe at 20 m/s**. Pressure signatures of leaks will therefore be weak in the released large-bore geometry, which is an input to leak sizing (S4) and the noise budget.
- Velocity grows to as much as 20.9 m/s along a pipe, so the 20 m/s limit must apply at the outlet.

## 6. Gas architecture

The versioned interface `gas_composition/0.1` is in `src/h2prism/gas_composition.py`:
- The pure-H2 table provider serves only x_H2 ≥ 1 − 10⁻⁶.
- Blends from 0.01 % to 20 % CH4 are refused, never extrapolated.
- Out-of-table (T, p) is refused.
- The mixture provider returns NOT_AVAILABLE with the reason.

H2–CH4 feasibility, recorded in `02_methodology/GAS_COMPOSITION_INTERFACE.md`:
- CoolProp 7.2.0 carries the GERG-2008 Methane–Hydrogen parameters (Kunz & Wagner 2012), but combines them with its own pure-fluid equations, so it is not identical to GERG-2008.
- The **default PT flash fails** for H2-rich blends (5–20 % CH4 at 300 K, 5 MPa).
- With an imposed gas phase the states evaluate.
- Mixture transport is unvalidated.
- No tables were generated; that needs an owner scope decision and a new authorization.
